The honest check

Is my roof a good fit?

Rejuvenation works on asphalt shingles that are drying out but still sound. It’s maintenance — not a repair.

Good candidate

  • Asphalt shingle roof
  • Shingles look faded, dry, or brittle
  • Granules collecting in gutters or at downspouts
  • No active leaks, soft spots, or missing shingles

Needs repair first

  • Active leaks or water stains on the ceiling
  • Missing, cracked, or curling shingles
  • Soft, sagging, or rotted decking
  • Failing flashing around chimneys or vents

Repair, replace, or rejuvenate?

Thinking about a new roof in San Angelo?

A lot of West Texas roofs get replaced years early because the shingles look tired, not because the roof is done. Here’s how roof rejuvenation, roof repair and roof replacement compare — and how to tell which one your roof needs.

Rejuvenate

Faded, dry or shedding granules — but sound

The spray-on treatment puts oils back into dried-out asphalt shingles so they flex instead of crack. No tear-off, no dumpster, and about $1.25–$1.75 per sq ft.

Repair

Leaks, missing or cracked shingles, bad flashing

Active roof leaks, storm-damaged or missing shingles and failing flashing need a roof repair first. Rejuvenation is maintenance — it doesn’t replace a repair.

Replace

Soft or rotted decking, roof at end of life

When the decking is going or the roof has truly failed, a roof replacement is the right call. A new asphalt shingle roof on 2,000 sq ft typically runs $8,000–$16,000 (national averages).

Questions

Straight answers

How much longer will my roof last?

On a structurally sound roof, one treatment typically adds about five years. How much you get depends on your roof’s age and condition — Joe will give you a straight read when he looks at it.

Will it stop a leak or fix missing shingles?

No. Rejuvenation brings dried-out shingles back to life, but it doesn’t stop active leaks, replace missing or cracked shingles, fix flashing, or put back granules that are already gone. Those need a repair first.

How is the price figured?

By the square footage of your roof — $1.25 to $1.75 per sq ft depending on how steep it is. Joe measures it and gives you the exact number before any work starts.

What kinds of roofs does it work on?

Asphalt shingle roofs. It isn’t meant for metal, tile, slate, or wood roofs.

When is the best time to treat my roof?

Before problems start. Once your shingles look dry or faded, or you notice granules in the gutters — while the roof is still sound — is the sweet spot.

Is roof rejuvenation cheaper than a roof replacement?

Much cheaper. On a 2,000 sq ft roof, rejuvenation runs about $2,500–$3,500, while a new asphalt shingle roof that size typically costs $8,000–$16,000 (national averages). It won’t save a roof that has failed, but on a sound roof it can push a replacement years down the road.

How do I know if I need roof repair or replacement instead?

Active leaks, water stains on the ceiling, missing or cracked shingles, soft or sagging decking, or failing flashing mean the roof needs a repair first — and some roofs are simply at the end of their life.
