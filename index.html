The honest check

Is my roof a good fit?

Rejuvenation works on asphalt shingles that are drying out but still sound. It’s maintenance — not a repair.

Good candidate

  • Asphalt shingle roof
  • Shingles look faded, dry, or brittle
  • Granules collecting in gutters or at downspouts
  • No active leaks, soft spots, or missing shingles

Needs repair first

  • Active leaks or water stains on the ceiling
  • Missing, cracked, or curling shingles
  • Soft, sagging, or rotted decking
  • Failing flashing around chimneys or vents

Questions

Straight answers

How much longer will my roof last?

On a structurally sound roof, one treatment typically adds about five years. How much you get depends on your roof’s age and condition — Joe will give you a straight read when he looks at it.

Will it stop a leak or fix missing shingles?

No. Rejuvenation brings dried-out shingles back to life, but it doesn’t stop active leaks, replace missing or cracked shingles, fix flashing, or put back granules that are already gone. Those need a repair first.

How is the price figured?

By the square footage of your roof — $1.25 to $1.75 per sq ft depending on how steep it is. Joe measures it and gives you the exact number before any work starts.

What kinds of roofs does it work on?

Asphalt shingle roofs. It isn’t meant for metal, tile, slate, or wood roofs.

When is the best time to treat my roof?

Before problems start. Once your shingles look dry or faded, or you notice granules in the gutters — while the roof is still sound — is the sweet spot.
